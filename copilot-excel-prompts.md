## Copilot Excel deep dive prompts


Using the data in this table, give me an overview of what this workbook contains, list the columns and what each one measures, and flag any columns with missing or inconsistent values.”

**Analyze Spreadsheet**

Using the FY24–FY25 spending table, rank departments by year-over-year change in total spend, show the top five increases and the top five declines with percentages, and put the results in a new sheet.”

Compare FY2025 against FY2024 procurement spend by agency and contract type. Highlight variances greater than 20%, flag transactions that are unusually high versus similar purchases, and explain the likely drivers in plain language.

**Formula creation**

Add a column that flags any contract whose end date falls within the next 90 days as ‘Expiring’, otherwise ‘Active’ — then explain the formula step by step so I can adjust the threshold later.

**Visualizations**

Summarize the top spending insights for executive leadership: create a chart comparing expenditures by region, list the three most significant trends, and write a five-sentence summary I can paste into a briefing.


**Good prompt creation**

Review agency procurement spending for FY25. Identify high-growth spending categories, unusual vendor concentrations, and departments exceeding budget forecasts. Summarize findings in a table and provide three recommendations for leadership review.
