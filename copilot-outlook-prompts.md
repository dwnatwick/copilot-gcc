## Copilot Outlook deep dive prompts

**Scenario: a 25-message procurement discussion thread.**

Summarize this email thread. Separate the response into key decisions, open issues, stakeholders involved, upcoming deadlines, and recommended next actions.


**Executive Summary**

Draft an executive summary of this email conversation for senior leadership. Focus on strategic impacts, budget concerns, risks, and required decisions.

**Vendor Communication**

Draft a professional response requesting clarification from the vendor while maintaining a collaborative and business-focused tone.

**Interagency Communication**

Rewrite this email for an interagency audience using concise, professional government language.

**Meeting Preparation**

Based on this email thread, prepare me for a meeting by providing background, key stakeholders, decisions required, risks and concerns, and questions I should ask.

**Action Item Tracking**

Review this conversation and identify all action items, owners, and deadlines in a table.

**Project Status Report**

Create a project status report based on this email thread including accomplishments, risks, blockers, and next steps.

**Procurement Evaluation**

Summarize vendor feedback received in this email thread and categorize comments into pricing, technical capability, schedule, and compliance concerns.

**Facilities Management**

Draft an update to building stakeholders explaining project progress, current risks, and next milestones in clear, non-technical language.

**Executive Briefing**

Create a one-page executive briefing from this conversation that includes key decisions, pending approvals, risks, and recommended actions.

**Constituent Service**

Draft a professional response acknowledging the concern, summarizing the issue, and outlining next steps while maintaining a customer-focused tone.

**Role based**

Act as a California state government program manager and draft an executive-level response.

**Output formatting**

Present the results as a table with owners, actions, due dates, and status.

**Tone management**

Rewrite this email to be concise, professional, and action-oriented.

**Risk Identification**

Identify potential project risks mentioned or implied within this conversation.

**Decision support**

Based on this thread, outline available options, advantages, disadvantages, and recommendations.

**Return from extended leave or vacation**

Summarize all email I received over the last two weeks while I was out of office. Group the summary by project and sender, identify anything that needs my attention, and list any decisions made in my absence. Then give me a prioritized list of follow-up actions in a table with the task, owner, due date, and urgency, starting with anything already overdue.

Follow up: Draft short replies to my three most urgent items.
