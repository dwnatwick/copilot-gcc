## Copilot Basic Prompts

### Starter prompts

Goal -> Context -> Source -> Expectations

I'm new to Microsoft 365 Copilot. Explain in plain language how you work across Outlook, Teams, Word, and Excel, and give me three examples of tasks you could take off my plate this week.

Acting as my project manager, summarize the attached status report in 5 bullets for an executive audience. Keep it under 120 words, avoid jargon, and end with the top risk and the decision you need from me.

Recap this Teams meeting. I was late to attend and need a brief overview of the project timeline. Did Adele Vance propose any changes? Include the project timeline in a table and any proposed changes from Adele.

### Outlook and Teams prompts

Summarize this email thread into the decisions made, open questions, and action items with owners. Then draft a polite reply confirming my two action items and asking for a due date.

### Word prompts

Draft a one-page onboarding guide for new team members covering our tools, meeting rhythms, and where to find documents. Use a friendly tone, short sections with headings, and a checklist for week one.

### PowerPoint prompts

Create a 6-slide presentation from this Word document for a 15-minute leadership update. Lead with the recommendation, keep 3 bullets per slide, and add speaker notes I can read aloud.

### Excel prompts

Analyze this sales table and highlight the top three trends by region and quarter. Add a column chart of quarterly totals, and explain the formula you would use to flag any month that dropped more than 10%.

### OneDrive and SharePoint prompts

Find the most recent version of our onboarding policy in the HR site, summarize what changed from the previous version, and list which teams are affected.

### OneNote prompts

Search my notebooks for notes about the budget planning meetings this quarter, summarize the key decisions, and list every follow-up item still assigned to me.

### Building good Copilot Habits

Based on my role and the meetings on my calendar this week, suggest three daily Copilot habits I could start Monday, with a ready-to-use prompt for each.
